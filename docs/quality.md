# Quality and comparison log

Weave produces a simplified experimental design; it has not yet been sewn or compared against another product. Do not interpret a successful PES readback as proof that a design will sew well.

For each comparison, record the source artwork, size, number of colors, fabric, stabilizer, machine, settings, stitch count, visible defects and a photograph of the result. Compare a simple logo, an illustration with thin details, transparent artwork, text, and a photograph at the same physical size in Weave and a reference tool such as Ink/Stitch or Hatch. Record file compatibility and notes from a real stitch-out separately from software previews. Store personal images and results outside the public repository.

Known limitations: simple corner-based background removal, no underlay or pull compensation, horizontal fill only, no satin stitching, and no physical validation. Fine details may disappear; designs can pucker, shift or have excessive jumps. Use a scrap test with suitable stabilizer and monitor the machine.
